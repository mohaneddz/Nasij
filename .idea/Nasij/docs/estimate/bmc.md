# Business Model Canvas (BMC) - Nasij

## Strategic Frame

Nasij is positioned as a **traceability and operational control platform** for Algeria's wool value chain, designed to cover the full process while prioritizing the collection stage where control failures begin [S01](./sources.md#s01).

## 9-Block Canvas

## 1) Customer Segments

### Primary Segment

- Wool-chain operators needing end-to-end visibility:
  - collection coordinators
  - depot managers
  - wash facilities
  - transformation operators

### Secondary Segment

- Upstream source actors (C1/C2/C3) who need predictable pickup and fair reconciliation [S19](./sources.md#s19).

### Institutional Segment

- Public and private stakeholders requiring chain-level reporting and compliance-grade records.

### Quantified context

- Agriculture contributes 13.96% of GDP [S09](./sources.md#s09).
- Rural share remains 24.72%, reinforcing the need for field-capable workflows [S08](./sources.md#s08).

## 2) Value Propositions

### For source actors

- Predictable collection missions.
- QR-backed proof of intake.
- Transparent lot status after handoff.

### For operations teams

- One lot identity from collection to commercialization.
- Real-time discrepancy alerts with owner assignment.
- Better yield/loss visibility by stage.

### For management and partners

- Audit-ready records and compliance support [S16](./sources.md#s16), [S18](./sources.md#s18).
- KPI dashboards for operational and financial decisions.

## 3) Channels

- Mobile app for field and source interactions (mobile-first justified by penetration and subscriptions) [S06](./sources.md#s06), [S07](./sources.md#s07), [S11](./sources.md#s11).
- Web dashboard for control tower and oversight.
- Institutional onboarding through direct partnerships and pilot programs.

## 4) Customer Relationships

- Operational onboarding with role-based SOP training.
- Embedded support for discrepancy and reconciliation workflows.
- Quarterly value reviews tied to measurable KPI outcomes.

## 5) Revenue Streams

- Per-kg traceability/processing fee [A08](./assumptions.md#a08).
- Per-mission logistics coordination fee [A09](./assumptions.md#a09).
- Discrepancy handling fee [A10](./assumptions.md#a10).
- Reporting/analytics add-ons for institutional partners [A31](./assumptions.md#a31).

## 6) Key Resources

- Role-based mobile app and backend APIs [S20](./sources.md#s20).
- Offline queue and replay infrastructure.
- QR issuance and scan tooling.
- Field operations team and playbooks.
- Compliance policies and audit controls [S16](./sources.md#s16), [S17](./sources.md#s17).

## 7) Key Activities

- Collection orchestration and lot indexing.
- Stage transition validation (D0/D1/D2/D3-D4).
- Alert monitoring and resolution tracking.
- Data governance and retention management.
- Partner reporting and service-level performance management.

## 8) Key Partners

- Collection and transport operators.
- Depot, wash, and transformation facilities.
- Institutional buyers and downstream offtake partners.
- Legal/compliance advisors for data governance.

## 9) Cost Structure

### Main cost buckets (base case)

- Field operations fixed cost [A21](./assumptions.md#a21).
- Platform engineering/cloud [A22](./assumptions.md#a22).
- Compliance and governance [A23](./assumptions.md#a23).
- Training and change management [A24](./assumptions.md#a24).
- Variable logistics and processing support [A25](./assumptions.md#a25), [A26](./assumptions.md#a26).

### Cost profile (base case)

- Year 1 operating envelope driven by rollout and onboarding.
- Annual cost escalation aligned with inflation context [S05](./sources.md#s05), [A27](./assumptions.md#a27).
- Margin improvement expected via discrepancy reduction and scale efficiency [A13](./assumptions.md#a13), [A38](./assumptions.md#a38).

## Strategic Coherence Check

- The model stays aligned to the challenge requirement: full-chain coverage with collection-first execution [S01](./sources.md#s01).
- Mobile-first approach is supported by national usage patterns [S11](./sources.md#s11), [S12](./sources.md#s12).
- Compliance posture is explicit due personal-data obligations [S16](./sources.md#s16), [S17](./sources.md#s17).
- Financial logic is assumption-driven and scenario-tested in the business plan [A33](./assumptions.md#a33)-[A36](./assumptions.md#a36).

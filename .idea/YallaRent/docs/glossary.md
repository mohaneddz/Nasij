# YallaRent Glossary

- `AY`: Academic Year.
- `TAM`: Total Addressable Market.
- `SAM`: Serviceable Available Market.
- `SOM`: Serviceable Obtainable Market.
- `P2P`: Peer-to-peer rental between students.
- `Attach Rate`: Share of orders that include an optional paid add-on.
- `Utilization Rate`: Share of inventory active in rentals during a period.
- `Loss/Damage Rate`: Share of inventory value lost or unrecoverable in a year.
- `Break-even`: First period where operating revenue covers operating costs.
- `Base Case`: Central forecast built from current assumptions.
- `Conservative Case`: Lower-demand scenario with slower scaling.
- `Upside Case`: Higher-demand scenario with faster scaling.
- `Protection Fee`: Optional fee for damage-coverage package.
- `Hub`: Campus-adjacent point for pickup, return, and inspection.
- `Listing Plan`: Paid subscription unlocking >3 active P2P listings.
- `Complementors`: External players that increase platform value without being direct competitors.

# YallaRent Services Overview

## 1. Core Rental Services

### 1.1 Company-Owned Inventory Rental
- Students rent essential items by day/week/month/semester.
- Initial catalog: desks, chairs, lamps, fans, mini-fridges, routers/repeaters, storage boxes, and study kits.
- Inventory is tagged for tracking and condition checks.

### 1.2 Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Student Listings
- Students can list up to 3 items for free.
- No transaction commission is charged on P2P rentals.
- Monetization is through listing-plan subscriptions for higher listing capacity and operational add-ons.

### 1.3 Rental Bundles
- Pre-configured offers for common needs:
  - Study Kit
  - Comfort Kit
  - Exam Kit
  - Dorm Starter Kit
  - Summer Kit

## 2. Trust and Safety Layer

### 2.1 Identity and Eligibility Verification
- Student identity checks at onboarding.
- Provider identity checks for P2P listing activation.
- Compliance with local personal data protection requirements [S22](./sources.md#s22), [S23](./sources.md#s23).

### 2.2 Deposits and Damage Workflow
- Deposits for selected categories.
- Optional damage protection add-on.
- Condition evidence at pickup and return.

### 2.3 Ratings and Dispute Handling
- Two-sided ratings for providers and renters.
- Escalation workflow for disputes and late returns.

## 3. Logistics Services

### 3.1 Pickup and Return Hubs
- Campus-adjacent points for handover, verification, and inspection.
- Hub operations scale by campus roll-out.

### 3.2 Paid Delivery / Pickup
- Optional room, dorm-gate, or campus-gate delivery.
- Time-slot based pickup at end of rental term.

### 3.3 Maintenance and Repair
- In-house triage plus partner repairs for reusable assets.
- Extends useful life and supports end-of-cycle resale.

## 4. Platform Services

### 4.1 Student App/Web Experience
- Browse catalog, compare prices, reserve availability, and manage active rentals.

### 4.2 Provider Dashboard
- Manage listings, availability, requests, and earnings for P2P providers.

### 4.3 Admin Operations Console
- Moderation, verification queue, inventory status, and dispute tooling.

## 5. Revenue-Linked Service Lines

- Company-owned rental revenue.
- Delivery fees.
- Damage protection fees.
- Subscription plans for high-volume providers.
- University partnership contracts.
- Repair/maintenance services.
- End-of-semester resale.

Revenue logic is detailed in [business-plan.md](./business-plan.md) and assumptions [A08-A37](./assumptions.md#a08).
